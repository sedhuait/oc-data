# Tata — monthly factsheets

Published by the AMC under SEBI's monthly disclosure requirement and
mirrored here unmodified, so that every fact recorded in
`products/funds/` can be checked against the document it was read from.

These PDFs are the AMC's copyright. They are stored byte-for-byte as
served, nothing is charged for them, and they come down on request.
What this project owns and gives away is the facts read out of them.

`documents.json` records the source URL, fetch date and sha256 of each file.
